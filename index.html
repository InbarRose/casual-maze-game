📖 Explorer's Field Guide

🎮 Movement & Controls Use WASD or Arrows to move. Press E or Space to interact with mechanisms. Press M to free-pan the map.
🔑 Color Keys & Gates Pick up Ruby (Red), Sapphire (Blue), and Emerald (Green) keys by walking over them to unlock matching colored gates.
🌉 3D Bridges & Ramps Ramps (▲) elevate you to Elevation 1 (Overhead Bridge) to cross over ground paths, or lower you back to Ground floor.
🌫️ Fog of War & Memory Vision radius dictates how far you see. In memory mode, maze layouts are revealed, while items stay hidden in darkness.
