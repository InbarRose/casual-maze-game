📐 Architect Handbook & Guides

🏗️ Maze Architect Basics

Design complex, multi-elevation labyrinths with locked gates, switches, and secret paths:

  • Draw Tools: Use Pencil to paint tiles, Fill for flood filling open areas, and Eraser to clear cells back to open floor.
  • Active Layer: Switch between Ground (0) and Overhead (1) to build multi-tier structures.
  • Visual Themes: Switch tilesets on the fly (Dungeon, Jungle, Molten, Snow, Cave, Sunset) with distinct color schemes and wall trim styles.

🌉 Bridges & Multi-Elevation Crossing

Bridges allow pathways to cross over and under each other without intersecting:

  • Bridge EW (B_EW): Ground level runs East-West under the bridge; Overhead level runs North-South across the bridge deck.
  • Bridge NS (B_NS): Ground level runs North-South under the bridge; Overhead level runs East-West across the bridge deck.
  • Directional Ramps (R_N, R_S, R_E, R_W): Smoothly transition the player between Ground (0) and Overhead (1). Step in the ramp's arrow direction to climb; step backwards to descend. Side entry is blocked to prevent floating.

🔑 Multi-Colored Keys & Locked Gates

Create non-linear puzzle progression with color-coded keycards and gates:

  • Place Gold, Ruby (Red), Sapphire (Blue), Emerald (Green), or Amethyst (Purple) keys.
  • Place matching locked doors. Use the Inspect Tool (🔍) to configure door requirements, customize colors, or link custom key IDs.

🕹️ Wiring Levers & Dynamic Mechanisms

Wire switches to open secret passages or raise barriers:

  • Place a Lever (🕹️), then inspect it with the Inspect Tool.
  • Click "+ Pick Target Tile" and click any tile on the canvas to link it.
  • Configure State A (Active / Pulled) and State B (Inactive / Rest) tile types (e.g. Floor ↔ Wall).
  • Click "⚡ Toggle Preview" to test the mechanism live right in the editor!

🧪 Testing, Custom Spawns & Inventory Preloading

Test your labyrinth efficiently without playing from the beginning every time:

  • Click "🧪 Test Config" or press T to open Playtest Configuration.
  • Choose a Custom Test Spawn Coordinate to drop your player directly into a specific chamber or high-elevation bridge.
  • Check Starting Inventory keys to preload keys into your backpack so you can test late-game doors immediately.
