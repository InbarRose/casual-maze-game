## Narrative Lore & Overview
Architect's Journal: The central dais is locked under royal purple seal. To break it, claim the Emerald Key in the southern cistern, open the eastern passage, and retrieve the Purple Keystone.

## Kishōtenketsu Architectural Structure
- **Ki (Introduction)**: Northwest entrance vestibule with North wall doorway (`wallDirection: "north"`), stairs ascending back into daylight, and Architect's Journal #3.
- **Shō (Development)**: Exploration through winding single-tile corridors (72% inertia, 0 dead space $\ge 4\times 4$) down the Western labyrinth to the cistern where the Emerald Key (`key_emerald_3`) rests.
- **Ten (Twist)**: The Emerald Gate (`door_emerald_3`) is the sole passage breaching the central barrier into the Eastern flank. In the East wing lies the Purple Keystone (`key_purple_3`) and a deceptive dead end with wall carving clues concealing the Prismatic Shard (`secret_gem_3`, 300 points) behind a secret wall (`SW`).
- **Ketsu (Resolution)**: Navigating back to the North Sanctum unlocks the Royal Purple Gate (`door_purple_3`), revealing the Exit Archway (`wall_archway`) with stairs descending into Chapter 1 Level 4.
