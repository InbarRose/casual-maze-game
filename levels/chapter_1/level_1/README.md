## Narrative Lore & Overview
Architect's Note #1: Every grand labyrinth begins with a single step. Beyond this entry vestibule lies the Golden Gate. Follow the winding corridors to recover the keystore; avoid dead-end traps.

## Kishōtenketsu Architectural Structure
- **Ki (Introduction)**: Grand architectural entrance door integrated into the North wall with visible stairs ascending up into the sunny world behind the player. Entry vestibule features the Architect's journal and an ancient stone carving.
- **Shō (Development)**: Authentic single-tile labyrinth corridors ($70\%$ inertia, $0$ dead space $\ge 4\times 4$) with branching corridors and blind turns. Exploring east reveals the locked Golden Gate; exploring south leads deeper into the maze wings.
- **Ten (Twist)**: A deceptive dead-end corridor in the southwest with a carved inscription hinting at hollow stone. Breaching the secret cracked wall (`SW`) discovers the secluded Sunstone sanctuary. Reaching the southeast wing recovers the Golden Key.
- **Ketsu (Resolution)**: Navigating back to unseal the Golden Gate unlocks the subterranean Exit Sanctum, featuring a grand carved archway with stone steps descending into the deep for Chapter 2.
